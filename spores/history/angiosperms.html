How we came to know the flowering plants

In 1879 Darwin called the sudden rise of the flowering plants an abominable mystery. These are the main attempts since then to say when they began, what they came from, how they spread, and what the end-Cretaceous impact did to them.

Each entry gives the claim in plain words, the kind of evidence behind it, and how it stands today. Click an entry to search for the paper on Google Scholar.
